From the phone · 1 / 4

Data Scraping from Phones

The prices, menus, and listings your team copies by hand are already on a phone.

I take that public data off mobile apps and websites and deliver it on a schedule: competitor prices and products, app listings, catalogs, reviews, and business leads you are allowed to use. This is the same kind of system I led at Yum! Brands, with a team of five and machines that start, collect, and stop. Private accounts and passwords stay out.

  • Competitor prices, products, and menus
  • Public app screens and website catalogs
  • Business leads from pages you may use
  • A feed that runs without someone watching it

After midnight · 2 / 4

AI Chatbot

Customers write in when the desk is empty. Someone still has to answer, book, and refund.

The chatbot lives on your website. It answers from the facts you give it, then takes the action: books the appointment, starts the refund, and opens the ticket. Your team sees what it did in the morning. It covers the hours staff are offline. It does not pretend a person is awake.

  • A chat widget on your website
  • Answers, appointments, and refunds
  • Tickets opened inside your rules
  • A record waiting for your team

Phone and desktop · 3 / 4

Website Modernization

An old site loses the customer who opened it on a phone.

I learn the business before touching the layout. Then the site is rebuilt for the phone and the desk, your existing content moves into it, pages get faster, and basic SEO is in place. You get a simple editor, so changing a sentence does not mean calling a developer.

  • The business understood before the redesign
  • Built for the phone and the desktop
  • Old content carried into the new layout
  • You edit the text after launch

Built for one job · 4 / 4

Custom Software Development

Some jobs do not fit a template, a plugin, or a no-code tool.

That is a product, scoped in writing and built for that one job: a desktop app, an iOS or Android app, a Telegram bot, a WhatsApp bot, or a complex React web app. The backend is part of the product. You get it running, with a handover that shows how to operate it.

  • Desktop apps and mobile apps
  • Telegram bots and WhatsApp bots
  • Complex React web applications
  • The backend included, then handed over running
